      FAQ

      Answers to the questions we get most

      How long does delivery take?

      Orders confirmed before 2pm are dispatched the same working day. Kampala and Wakiso receive next day, upcountry takes two to three working days. You get a tracking link by SMS as soon as the parcel leaves us.

      Do I pay delivery?

      Delivery is free on orders over the free-delivery threshold shown in your cart summary. Below that a flat rate applies and it is calculated before you pay, so there is nothing added at the last step.

      What is your returns policy?

      You have 30 days from delivery to return a product in original condition with all accessories. We arrange and pay for the pickup, and refunds are processed within three working days of the item reaching us.

      How do warranty claims work?

      Open a claim from your order page with a short video of the fault and the invoice attached. We arrange collection and a replacement, usually within a week. You do not need to find a service centre or argue about the receipt.

      Are the products genuine?

      Every product is sourced through authorised channels and serial numbers are logged against your order. If a unit fails inspection it never reaches you, and if a product is ever found not to be genuine we refund in full and close the supplier account.

      Do you do trade-ins or bulk orders?

      Yes to both. Send the device details and your requirements through the form above choosing "Trade-in or bulk order", and we will come back with a valuation or a quote within one working day.
